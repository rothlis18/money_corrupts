**2. Capital Allocation Mechanisms**

**2.1 Regulatory Capture**

Non-governmental global philanthropic foundations often engage in regulatory capture by influencing policy decisions through targeted grants and partnerships. This is achieved through the following mechanisms:

- **Grants and Funding**: Foundations provide substantial funding to organizations and projects that align with their strategic objectives, thereby influencing policy priorities.
- **Influence Networks**: By building extensive networks of influencers, including policymakers, academics, and industry leaders, foundations can shape regulatory environments to their advantage.
- **Public-Private Partnerships (PPPs)**: PPPs allow foundations to leverage public funds and resources while maintaining control over project outcomes, ensuring alignment with their strategic goals.

**2.2 Intellectual Property Monopolies**

Foundations establish intellectual property monopolies through the following strategies:

- **Patent and Licensing Agreements**: By securing patents and licensing agreements, foundations can control the dissemination and commercialization of technologies, ensuring long-term revenue streams.
- **Research and Development (R&D) Funding**: Foundations fund R&D projects that lead to breakthroughs in various fields, thereby creating intellectual property that can be monetized.
- **Open Access Initiatives**: While ostensibly promoting open access, foundations can use these initiatives to establish control over the dissemination of knowledge and technologies.

**2.3 Infrastructure Dependencies**

Foundations secure multi-year infrastructure dependencies through:

- **Long-Term Grants**: Providing long-term grants for infrastructure projects ensures that foundations maintain control over the project outcomes and can influence future policy decisions.
- **Technical Assistance and Training**: By providing technical assistance and training, foundations can ensure that local institutions are dependent on their expertise and resources.
- **Capacity Building**: Foundations invest in capacity building initiatives that create a dependency on their continued support and influence.
