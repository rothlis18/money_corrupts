**Endowment IRR Expansion:**

The Internal Rate of Return (IRR) expansion for tax-sheltered endowment assets can be calculated using the following formula:

\[ IRR = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{t=1}^{n} \frac{R_t}{(1 + r)^t} \]

Where:
- \( IRR \) is the Internal Rate of Return
- \( n \) is the number of periods
- \( R_t \) is the return in period \( t \)
- \( r \) is the discount rate

For instance, if an endowment achieves returns of 8%, 10%, and 12% over three years, with a discount rate of 5%, the IRR would be:

\[ IRR = \frac{1}{3} \left( \frac{8}{(1.05)^1} + \frac{10}{(1.05)^2} + \frac{12}{(1.05)^3} \right) \approx 9.18\% \]

\[ \Delta V = \frac{\Delta M}{M} \times 100\% \]

- \( M \) is the initial money supply

Assuming a PPP crowds out $10 billion in sovereign domestic investments, and the initial money supply \( M \) is $100 billion, the devaluation would be:

\[ \Delta V = \left( \frac{10}{100} \right) \times 100\% = 10\% \]
